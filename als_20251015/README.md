# als_20251015 — radiation damage series (1 dataset)

This directory contains X-ray Bragg and diffuse scattering datasets from one Mac1 crystal generated as part of Prism, Astera Institute's effort to develop reproducible methods for generating high quality, diffuse scattering data. This dataset resulted from experimenting with exposure conditions and is considered poor quality due to radiation damage.

Beamline: 8.3.1, ALS (Advanced Light Source), Berkeley, CA — October 15, 2025

[Logbook entry](https://diffuse.science/logbook/beamtime/20251015-17-als/#humidity-box-mac1-crystal-7)

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mac1_7 | 13.191 | 0.014 | 89.242 | 40.221 |

`mac1_7/crystal/` contains the raw Bragg + diffuse frames; `mac1_7/background/` contains the matching background images.
